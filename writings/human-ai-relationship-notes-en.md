## Thesis (2026-10-13)

**Direction: work with AI — and be better at it than everyone else.**

Three things, unpacked: clear intent — know what you want, so you can spot when the AI is wrong; a verification system — cross-check across models + test it yourself, never get led by the nose; taste — knowing what's actually good versus what merely looks right. Multiply the three together and you get agency × taste.
