## Thesis (2026-10-13)

**Direction: work with AI and do it better than others.**

Three things, unpacked: clear intent — know what you want, so you can spot when AI is wrong; a verification system — cross-check across models and test by hand, so you are never led by the nose; and taste — knowing what is good and what only looks right. Multiply the three and you get agency × taste.
